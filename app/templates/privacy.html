Your data and privacy

What this alpha stores

Your email, password hash, selected permit and weekly class schedule. We do not collect your location, license plate, payment details or university credentials. Use a unique password and only test information during the alpha.

Your controls

Signed-in students can download their own data and permanently delete their account from the dashboard. Deletion removes the live account, classes and login sessions. Administrators cannot delete their own accounts through the student interface.

Access and retention

Students see only their own schedule. Administrators manage parking lots and review lot-change history. Sessions expire after one hour and sign-out revokes the current session. Authentication counters contain keyed hashes, not raw email addresses or IP addresses. Expired counters and sessions are removed on the next authentication attempt. Account data stays until you delete it. Administrator history is retained for the alpha.

Hosting limits

The hosting provider may keep access logs and backups separately. Account deletion removes live application data, but does not immediately erase historical backups. A deployed release requires HTTPS and a restricted, persistent database. This alpha is not connected to official university parking systems.
